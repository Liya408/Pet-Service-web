Pet Tips

A little advice, fresh from our vets

Bite-sized guidance to keep your companion healthy, calm, and happy between visits.

🦴
Nutrition

Reading pet food labels

Look for a named protein source first on the ingredient list, and avoid foods that list "meat by-products" as the top item.

🚶
Exercise

Daily walks, rain or shine

Most adult dogs need 30–60 minutes of activity a day — split it into two walks to ease joint strain and boredom.

🦷
Hygiene

Brushing teeth, the easy way

Start with a pet-safe toothpaste on your finger before introducing a brush — three short sessions a week make a big difference.

Our Story

Built by pet lovers, for pet lovers

Tail & Whisker started in a small converted garage with one vet, one groomer, and a very patient golden retriever named Biscuit. Over a decade later, we're still that same neighborhood clinic — just with a bigger team and a longer waiting-room treat jar.

Every member of our staff is a pet owner too, so we treat your animals the way we'd want ours treated: gently, honestly, and without unnecessary procedures.
